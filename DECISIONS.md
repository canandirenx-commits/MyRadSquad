# MyRadSquad — Decision Log

Case: PlusMinusOne Product Designer, Case A. Deadline: Fri, Nov 13, 2026.
Owner of all product/design decisions: Canan. Build partner: Claude (Claude Code).

## Flow
Welcome → 13+ age gate → "Why are you here?" → personalized payoff → Paywall A (trial or close) → Home → Squad chat → Spark (activator view) → Spark (squad member view)
Paywall B = static screen. A "View as: Me / Friend" toggle lets reviewers switch between the two AI states.

## Decisions

| # | Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI feature | **Spark**: the AI posts a question, video suggestion, quiz, poll or mini-game into the squad feed | Brief insight: "a squad feels dead when it's quiet" |
| 2 | Auto trigger | Fires after a quiet period that **the activator picks in setup** (2h / 4h / 8h) | Gives control to the person who turned it on |
| 3 | Manual Sparks | The activator can also send a Spark any time by choosing a topic and content type | Lets Sparks start conversations, not only fill silence |
| 4 | Consent | **One member turns it on, everyone sees it**: a visible "X turned on Spark" card plus a "what it can see" sheet | Openness without the friction of a vote |
| 5 | Anti-spam | Any member can **swap or cancel** a Spark; Sparks are labeled with an AI icon | The element that prevents "spammy" |
| 6 | Visual separation | **(Updated by #46)** Spark cards look completely different from member messages: the **aurora gradient** (border, glow and the ✦ icon) appears **only on Spark cards** | The element that prevents "creepy": AI never passes as a friend |
| 7 | Notifications | **Batched**: one updating notification per Spark ("Ece + 3 others answered: your turn"); answers stay hidden until you answer | Keeps a 12-person squad from turning into noise |
| 8 | Personalization | Each member's "why are you here?" answer sets the topic of **Sparks sent in their direction** (study → study quizzes, sports → sports games). It also personalizes the pre-paywall payoff screen. | Every onboarding answer visibly matters |
| 9 | Arrival | The new user arrives **through a friend's invite** and lands in an existing squad | Home already feels alive two minutes after onboarding |
| 10 | Visual direction | **(Updated by #46)** Raw/intimate + dark/glowy, photo- and content-first, close to BeReal. SF Pro, iOS type scale, iOS radii. Near-black UI. **Aurora gradient (violet → cyan → lime) only on Spark cards.** Primary CTA buttons are **one solid color** (violet, `--color-accent`). | Distinctive, and the gradient means "AI" and nothing else |
| 11 | Paywall A | 3-day trial, **yearly preselected** ($29.99/yr ≈ $0.58/wk), weekly available, can be closed | Better long-term value |
| 12 | A/B test *(replaced by #62)* | B = **weekly preselected**; nothing else changes | A clean, single-variable test |
| 13 | Deciding metric | **Revenue per paywall viewer at day 30**, not trial starts | Weekly will almost certainly win on trial starts, so that metric would mislead |
| 14 | Scope | Voice rooms and video messages appear only as **visual hints** (not tappable) | Time goes to the four required flows |

| 14b | Icons | **No emoji in the UI.** One icon family: **Phosphor**, in a **single style: regular line** (closest to SF Symbols' default "regular" weight) on every screen. No fill, bold or duotone. Selection is shown by color, border and background, not by icon style. Only exception: **play/pause are filled**, as on iOS (media controls). The Spark mark and status bar glyphs are a brand mark and system elements, not icons. Emoji stays only inside user-written messages. | One consistent icon language across the product |
| 15 | "Why are you here?" screen | One screen, two sections: **"What's your squad for?"** (Study / Stay close / Share moments) + **"What are you into?"** chips (Sports, Music, Gaming, Fashion, Movies, Memes) | Purpose shapes the payoff; interests set Spark topics. Adds no extra onboarding screen. |
| 16 | Auto Spark topics | **Shared interests first**: favor topics several members picked | The most inclusive moment for the whole squad. Less common interests still appear through manual Sparks (#3). |
| 17 | Spark labels | Auto Sparks show **only a topic chip** ("⚽ Sports"); manual ones show **"Ece sent a Spark · 📚 Study"**. Onboarding answers are never revealed. | Avoids the creepy feeling of "the app told everyone what I picked" |
